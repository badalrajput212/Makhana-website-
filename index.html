OUR ROOTS & ORIGIN

ROOTED IN MITHILA.
CRAFTED FOR TODAY.

In the nutrient-dense wetlands of Mithila, Bihar, makhana has been cultivated with sacred devotion for over two thousand years.

Known botanically as Euryale Ferox, these precious seeds grow deep inside serene freshwater ponds shielded by giant spiny lotus leaves. For generations, traditional Mallah harvesting families have dived into the morning waters to collect these organic black seeds from pond beds.

Our brand celebrates this profound agricultural heritage while presenting it in a modern, refined format. We bypass exploitative middlemen to work directly with farmer collectives, ensuring fair wages, ecological pond restoration, and grade-A jumbo harvests roasted to crunch perfection.
